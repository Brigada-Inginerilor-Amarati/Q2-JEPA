# Chess: CNN vs ViT twin vs HQViT (negative result)

Chess positions with 3 equally good engine moves ([candidate datasets spec §4](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-q2jepa-candidate-datasets.md)),
test split (128 positions), 128 prior draws each, seeds 0–2. Preset `+experiment=chess` (64×64, `input_norm=l2`).
The clip is three views of one board, so this is not a video world model. Schedules tuned on chess dev (80 positions,
seed 0), selected by coverage: `+tune=s1` for all three encoders. The decision to tune was made after seeing seed-0 test
metrics (spec §4.3), and those seed-0 test runs are reused as grid rows because tuning kept `s1`. HQViT
trains with `quantum.hqvit_train=torch` (exact torch simulation) and is evaluated through the CUDA-Q circuits.

**Reproduce:** `python scripts/run_grid.py --bench chess --stage tune`, then `--stage grid --seeds 0 1 2`. One run is
`python -m q2jepa.train +experiment=chess +tune=s1 eval.split=test encoder=<cnn|vit|hqvit>
prior=<softmax_categorical|entangled_qcbm> seed=<0|1|2> quantum=gpu trainer.device=cuda`, plus
`quantum.hqvit_train=torch` for HQViT. Data: `python scripts/fetch_chess.py`. Reference rows: `python scripts/chess_baselines.py --split test` (seed 0), in
the commit that adds this page.
**Commits:** seed 0 (all rows) and HQViT + Entangled-QCBM seed 2 at **eef990e**; every other run at **b2d62d3**.
Between the two, `src/` and `conf/` gained only TaxiBJ code (with its registration and `flow_metrics`) and changed one
`chess.yaml` comment. Seed 0 took its schedule from MovingMNIST's tuning, which is also `s1`: same overrides.
**Platform:** Colab T4, `nvidia` target. Some runs were resumed from checkpoints.

**Reading.**
- On validity, coverage and ES every row is worse than a random legal move (validity 13.1, coverage 98.7, ES 1.01), so
  no row does better than knowing the rules. The rows beat random
  squares on validity, coverage, TV and ES, but are worse on minADE (0.98–1.02 vs 0.78), where diverse samples help.
- HQViT vs its classical twin: with Softmax-Categorical HQViT is above by slightly more than one seed s.d. (coverage
  higher on every seed); with Entangled-QCBM they are level. With 3 seeds, and every row below a random legal move,
  this is no evidence of a real difference. Both have a readout ceiling of ~45%.
- No prior effect: Softmax-Categorical and Entangled-QCBM are within the larger seed s.d. for every encoder.
- CNN is bimodal over seeds: seed 0 readout validity is 0.11–0.12, seeds 1–2 are 0.88–0.90, with identical configs.
  The dev tuning run of seed 0 at b2d62d3 (`cnn-s1`, readout validity 0.15) was also low, so the commit is not the
  likely cause; the cause was not investigated. Read the CNN rows per seed, not as a mean.
- The HQViT `s1` choice rests on 0.4 coverage points over `s3` on 80 dev positions.
- `data.chess.move_state` maps any e1h1/e1a1 move to castling, so a rook or queen move on those squares is scored as
  castling, in the models' targets and in the random-legal row. Not counted; expected to be rare.

Most sampled moves are illegal in every row. A random legal move scores 100% by construction; two random squares score at
most 5.3% (a position has at most 218 legal moves of 4,096 pairs), so the models learned part of the rules. ViT and HQViT
are within the larger seed s.d. of each other. CNN seeds 1–2 (30–36%) are above every ViT and HQViT run; seed 0 is the
low-readout run, so read the CNN per seed. Three runs were checked against their recorded validity (CNN Softmax seed 2
0.11017, ViT Softmax seed 1 0.02374, HQViT QCBM seed 0 0.05176): the share of draws equal to a true move is identical.
The other 15 use the same code path and were not checked. All true moves of the test positions are legal.
